Most data-leak programmes focus on e-mail, USB drives and cloud uploads. Meanwhile, the easiest way to take a confidential document out of a building is to photograph the screen.

## Why traditional controls miss it

Data-loss prevention, classification and rights-management tools watch files and networks. A phone camera, a printout or a screen shared in the wrong meeting never touches them. When a photo surfaces, there is often no way to prove where it came from.

## Make every copy carry its owner

- **Screen watermarks** show the user, device and time over sensitive applications, so any photo carries its source.
- **File and print watermarks** add the same details to documents, PDF exports and printouts.
- **Screen and application control** block unauthorized screenshots, screen sharing and capture tools, and log every attempt.

## Deterrence first, forensics second

Visible watermarks change behaviour: people think twice when their name is on the screen. And if a leak does happen, investigators can trace it to a user and device in minutes, with evidence that supports legal and HR action.

## Regulation points the same way

NCA data controls address secure handling of data in meetings, screen capture on document-processing devices and watermarking to trace users or devices <Verify />. Watermarking is becoming an expectation, not an extra.
