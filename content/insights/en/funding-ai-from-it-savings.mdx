Every AI strategy eventually meets the same question from finance: where does the money come from? For many organizations, part of the answer is already inside the IT budget.

## The hidden cost of the software estate

Licences renewed for people who left, subscriptions nobody uses, oversized cloud instances and true-up penalties after vendor audits all add up. Without a trusted inventory, nobody can challenge them.

## Three steps to free budget

1. **Discover everything.** Automated discovery across data centres, cloud and endpoints creates one normalized inventory.
2. **Match ownership to usage.** Mapping entitlements against what is deployed and used shows where you are over-licensed and where you carry audit risk.
3. **Reclaim and renegotiate.** Reclaim unused licences, right-size cloud spend and enter renewals with evidence.

## What it can be worth

In production deployments, organizations typically reduce software costs by 20% or more, with payback in around six months <Verify />. Results depend on the size and maturity of the estate.

## Turning savings into AI

Ring-fence part of the savings for a fixed-scope AI proof of value. Finance sees a self-funding plan, and the AI programme starts with a clear business case.
